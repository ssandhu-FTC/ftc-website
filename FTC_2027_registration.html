4. Safety, Consent & Conduct

Legal review required before launch. These are acknowledgments, not a complete liability waiver. FTC should obtain a California attorney-reviewed participation agreement and review its insurance and safety policies before collecting registrations.

Voluntary participation and risks: My child’s participation in FTC basketball is voluntary. I understand that practices, games, tournaments and related activities involve physical exertion and foreseeable risks such as falls, collisions, sprains, fractures, concussion, and other injuries. I understand that FTC cannot guarantee an injury-free experience. This acknowledgment does not waive any rights or protections that cannot legally be waived.

Emergency assistance: If my child experiences an emergency during an FTC activity, I authorize FTC personnel to contact emergency medical services, notify me or the listed emergency contact, and seek appropriate emergency assistance if I cannot be reached promptly. Medical professionals determine treatment under applicable law. This is not a substitute for any additional medical consent required by law.

Player and parent code of conduct: We agree to treat teammates, coaches, officials, opponents, volunteers and families with respect. Bullying, harassment, threats, fighting, discriminatory conduct, and prohibited substances at FTC youth activities are not allowed. FTC may impose proportionate discipline, including removal from activities, under its policies.

5. Additional Parent / Guardian Permissions

Photo & Social Media Permission

FTC personnel may photograph or record players at practices, games, tournaments, and FTC events. With your permission, FTC may use identifiable photos or videos of your child on FTC’s official website, social media accounts, and program promotional materials. FTC will not intentionally publish a child’s contact information with these images. Choosing “No” will not affect registration or participation. You may contact info@ftchoops.org to withdraw permission for future uses; content already shared or reposted may not be fully removable.

Tournament Travel Permission

FTC may offer supervised transportation or accompaniment to tournaments without a parent present. This question only indicates your willingness to consider that arrangement. A “Yes” is not authorization for any specific trip. FTC will request separate written authorization before each trip, stating destination, dates, transportation arrangements, designated adults, emergency contact procedures and any required travel information. Choosing “No” will not affect regular program participation.

Medication & Emergency Policy

FTC personnel generally do not administer or dispense medication. If a child needs medication assistance during a tournament, a parent or guardian should accompany the child unless an appropriate arrangement is agreed in advance and permitted by law. FTC will consider applicable disability accommodations and emergency-care requirements. This policy does not restrict contacting emergency services or assistance permitted or required by law. Families should contact FTC privately in advance about necessary accommodations rather than entering detailed medical information in this form.

6. Registration & Monthly Payment

FTC 2027 Year-Round Basketball Program

Monthly fee: $117 per player. This is a recurring monthly program fee, not a one-time annual fee. Payments are made separately by PayPal, Zelle, or Cash; submitting this form does not charge you automatically.

Payment terms to confirm before launch: FTC must publish its payment due date, start-month/proration rules, cancellation notice, refunds, and missed-payment policy. Do not accept payments until these are finalized.

Cash payment: Arrange payment with an authorized FTC representative. Request a dated receipt showing the player name, amount, payment month, and receipt number. FTC will mark the payment received only after an authorized representative verifies it.

Payment instructions: FTC will provide its dedicated PayPal registration payment link, official Zelle payment details, and cash-payment arrangements before collecting payments. Do not use the charitable donation page for program fees. Include the player’s full name and “FTC 2027 Monthly Basketball” in the memo when supported. FTC verifies payments separately.
